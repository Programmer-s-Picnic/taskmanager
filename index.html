Where is the data stored?

Tasks: Saved in localStorage in the current browser. They do not automatically synchronise between devices and can be lost when browser storage is cleared. Hello World: Fetched from GoDaddy over HTTPS after you configure its published address. The supplied backend deliberately has no public write API, database, or login.

This distinction is essential for learning safely: we connect the two environments first, then introduce persistent, authenticated task APIs in the advanced chapters.
